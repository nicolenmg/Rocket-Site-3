The Solar System

Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet in our solar system.

Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun.
It is often called Earth's "sister planet."

Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun.
It is the only astronomical object known to harbor life.

Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun.
It is often called the "Red Planet."

Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun.
It is the largest in our solar system.

Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun.
It is known for its prominent ring system.

Uranus

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun.
It has a unique sideways rotation.
